# Project Goal

Freelancers in DCI are required to send a monthly invoice so that they can get paid. The process of generating this invoice is not that smooth. We need to copy all tasks from a google doc to another google doc containing the invoice format. With a lot of tasks, this can take about 2 hours to complete. And most at times, the format is not appealing to the eyes.

Here, I present to you the `DCI Invoicer`. It allow us to generate the invoice automatically, with a good format that is appealing to the eyes.
